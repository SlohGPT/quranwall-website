# Pricing, QuranWall

QuranWall is an iPhone app that puts an ayah of the Quran on the Lock Screen wallpaper and a Home Screen widget.
It is free to download, with an in-app subscription.

This file states no prices on purpose: they change and differ by region. The App Store
shows the exact price and billing period before you subscribe, in your local currency.

## Plans

- Download: free to download, with an in-app subscription
- In-app subscription: auto-renewing. The App Store shows the price and billing period before you subscribe.
- Cancel or manage a subscription any time in your Apple ID settings (Apple subscriptions page).

## Platforms

- iPhone, iOS 15.0 or later (App Store listing)
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quran-verse-lock-screen/id6781802080?pt=128303795&ct=qw-web-pricing&mt=8
